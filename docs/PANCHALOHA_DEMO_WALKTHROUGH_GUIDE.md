* **How to open:** Click **Shop All** in the header or click **View All** on any section.
* **What to Show:**

  2. **Sort Dropdown:** Sort by *Curated*, *Newest*, *Price: Low to High*, or *Price: High to Low*.

## Part 4: Interactive Product Detail Page (PDP)

* **How to open:** Click on any jewellery card (e.g., *blooming Light Weight butterfly Gold Necklace*).
* **Key Features to Demonstrate:**
  1. **Multi-Angle Studio Gallery:** Click on the thumbnails on the left to switch camera angles.
  2. **Inspect Zoom:** Click the main photo to open the fullscreen high-resolution inspection modal.
  3. **Karat / Noble Metal Selector:** Switch between `22KT Yellow Gold`, `18KT Rose Gold`, and `Platinum 950`.
  4. **Chain Length Selector:** Switch between `16"`, `18"`, and `20"` inches.
  5. **Quantity Controls:** Adjust quantity with `+` and `-`.
  6. **Pincode Delivery Check:** Enter any 6-digit Indian pincode (e.g. `560001` or `110001`) and click **Verify**. It displays guaranteed insured delivery dates.
  7. **Tabbed Artisanal Specifications:** Switch between `Specifications`, `Hallmark & Certification`, and `Insured Shipping`.
  8. **Harmonious Pairings:** 4 curated matching products at the bottom of the page.

## Part 5: Shopping Cart & Checkout Suite (`#cart`)

* **How to open:** Click **"Add to Bag"** on any product, then click **"View Bag & Checkout"** (or click the Bag icon in the header).
* **Key Features to Demonstrate:**
  1. **Item Overview:** Shows thumbnail, title, selected metal karat, selected size, and unit price.
  2. **Quantity Modifiers:** Increase or decrease item count; the subtotal updates automatically.
  3. **Free Insured Transit Progress Bar:** Orders above ₹50,000 unlock 100% complimentary armoured transport.
  4. **Promo Voucher Box:** Type `PANCHALOHA10` and click **Apply**. An instant 10% discount is deducted from the total.
  5. **Statutory 3% GST Tax Breakdown:** Displays the Base Price, 1.5% CGST, 1.5% SGST, and the final grand total.
  6. **Escrow Checkout Button:** Click **"Proceed to 100% Insured Checkout"**.

## Part 6: Order Confirmation & Indian GST Tax Invoice (`#order-confirmation`)

* **How to open:** Automatically opens upon placing an order, or by clicking checkout in the cart.
* **Key Features to Demonstrate:**
  1. **Consignment Tracker:** Displays unique Order ID (e.g., `PAN-2026-8941`) and live armored transit timeline.
  2. **Insured Delivery Destination:** Shows verified customer name and shipping address.
  3. **Statutory Indian Tax Invoice (THE HIGHLIGHT):**
     * Click the button **"View Statutory Tax Invoice"**.
     * **What Happens:** Opens an official **Form GST INV-1 Indian Jewellery Tax Invoice Modal**.
     * **Point Out These Details:**
       - Company GSTIN (`29AABCP1234M1Z2`)
       - HSN Code `7113` (Precious Metal Jewellery)
       - BIS Hallmarking License Number (`CM/L-8765432`)
       - Itemized Table with Base Value, 1.5% CGST, 1.5% SGST
       - Grand Total written in legal words (e.g. *"Rupees Seventy-Seven Thousand Four Hundred Fifty-Two Only"*)
       - Click **"Print Tax Invoice (A4)"**: It opens the browser print dialog formatted perfectly for standard A4 paper without background scrolling.

## Part 7: Secret Admin Portal (`#admin`)

* **How to open:** Add `#admin` to the URL in the browser address bar (e.g., `localhost:5173/#admin`).
* **What to Say:** *"This is our secret, standalone management panel. Store managers don't need a backend server to update product information."*
* **Key Features to Demonstrate:**
  1. **Live Catalogue Overview:** Shows total pieces, active categories, and statutory GST status.
  2. **Edit Any Product:** Click **"Edit Product"** on any item.
     * Change the **Title** or **Description**.
     * Change the **Base Price**: Watch the **3% GST** and **Final Retail Price** calculate automatically in real time!
     * Click **"Save Changes"**.
  3. **Instant Storefront Reflection:** Click **"Back to Storefront"**. The product's new price, title, and description are immediately live on the homepage and catalogue!
  4. **Persistent Storage:** Explain that edits persist in browser `localStorage`.
  5. **Factory Reset:** If you want to undo your demo edits, click **"Reset Catalog"** to restore original factory data.
